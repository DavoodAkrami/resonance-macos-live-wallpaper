## Atelier collection

Ten still wallpapers explore carved limestone, lacquer, brushed suede, and gold leaf. Ten new live wallpapers add slow movement in glass, ceramic, and silk. Resonance now includes **65 wallpapers**: 10 still, 35 ambient live, and 20 music reactive.

**New installation:** Download **Resonance-2.3.0-macOS.dmg**, open it, and drag Resonance into Applications.

**Updating from 2.2.1 or earlier:** Quit Resonance from its menu bar, drag the new version into Applications, choose Replace, then reopen it. Your existing preferences and favorites carry over. Version 2.3.0 adds **Check for Updates…** to the menu bar for future releases.

Requires macOS 14.2 or later and Metal. The universal app includes Apple Silicon and Intel binaries. Tested on Apple Silicon. This community build is ad-hoc signed and has no Apple notarization, so Gatekeeper may block its first launch.
